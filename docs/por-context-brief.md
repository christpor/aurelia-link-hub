## Project classification

This is a **marketing and social-commerce link hub**. It serves visitors arriving from social channels and presents campaigns, priority destinations, products, editorial links, social links, and a newsletter capture surface. It is not a Christpor portfolio, resume, About, or Contact page; no personal profile card or contact information is in scope.
